## Usage

<code>[TypeTheoryRules]()[*name*]</code> gives the rules of the named type theory: `"CalculusOfInductiveConstructions"`, the rules of the prelude, or `"MartinLofTypeTheory"`, which has no universe of propositions.

<code>[TypeTheoryRules]()[*name*, *setting* -> *value*, ...]</code> gives those rules with some settings changed.

## Details & Options

- The rules are what the kernel allows and computes; the declarations of an environment are what it assumes. Every environment carries the rules it was started with and checks each declaration and term by them.
- <code>[TypeTheoryEnvironment]()[*rules*]</code> gives the environment with no constants that follows *rules*, and <code>[TypeTheoryEnvironment]()[*rules*, {*declaration1*, ...}]</code> extends it. Every environment made from it follows the same rules, and <code>[Information]()[*env*, "Rules"]</code> gives them.
- The setting `"Prop"` says whether <code>[TypeUniverse]()[0]</code> is a universe of propositions: impredicative, with proof irrelevance, K-like reduction and elimination of subsingletons into any universe.
- The setting `"RewriteRules"` says whether [TypeTheoryRewriteRule]() declarations may give an axiom computation rules.
- The setting `"Quotients"` says whether [TypeTheoryQuotients]() may declare the quotient types of Lean's kernel, whose lift computes on a class. The calculus of inductive constructions has them, as Lean's kernel does; without them it is the theory of Rocq's kernel. They need Prop.
- Without Prop, every universe level must be at least 1 whatever its universe parameters are: <code>[TypeUniverse]()[*u* + 1]</code>, which shows as `Type u`, not <code>[TypeUniverse]()[*u*]</code>. A recursor then eliminates into `Type u`, and a theorem may state any type.
- Without Prop, no two proofs of an equality are made equal, which univalence needs: <code>[TypeTheoryEnvironment]()["HomotopyTypeTheory"]</code> follows `"MartinLofTypeTheory"` with rewrite rules.
- Recursors are generated by the rules, so the declarations [Normal]() gives rebuild an environment under its own rules but need not check under others.

## Basic Examples

The prelude follows the calculus of inductive constructions:

```wl
Information[TypeTheoryEnvironment[], "Rules"]["Name"]
```

<!-- => "CalculusOfInductiveConstructions" -->

---

Martin-Löf type theory has no Prop:

```wl
TypeTheoryRules["MartinLofTypeTheory"]["Prop"]
```

<!-- => False -->

## Scope

An environment of those rules, where a theorem states a type:

```wl
units = TypeTheoryEnvironment[TypeTheoryRules["MartinLofTypeTheory"], {
    TypeTheoryInductiveType[Unit, TypeUniverse[1], {UnitIntroduction -> Unit}],
    TypeTheoryTheorem[UnitInhabited, Unit, UnitIntroduction]
}]
```

<!-- => TypeTheoryEnvironment summary box: 4 constants -->

Its constants:

```wl
Keys[units]
```

<!-- => {Unit, UnitIntroduction, InductiveRecursor[Unit], UnitInhabited} -->

Its recursor eliminates into `Type u`:

```wl
TypeTheoryCheck[TypeTheoryTerm[InductiveRecursor[Unit], units]]
```

<!-- => ∀_m∶Unit → Type u m[UnitIntroduction] → ∀_t∶Unit m[t] -->

---

Rules with a setting changed:

```wl
TypeTheoryRules["MartinLofTypeTheory", "RewriteRules" -> True]["RewriteRules"]
```

<!-- => True -->

---

The calculus of inductive constructions has quotient types, and Martin-Löf type theory does not:

```wl
{TypeTheoryRules["CalculusOfInductiveConstructions"]["Quotients"], TypeTheoryRules["MartinLofTypeTheory"]["Quotients"]}
```

<!-- => {True, False} -->

## Possible Issues

Without Prop, no universe may be Prop, for any value of its universe parameters:

```wl
TypeTheoryEnvironment[TypeTheoryRules["MartinLofTypeTheory"], {TypeTheoryAxiom[AnySort, TypeUniverse[u]]}]
```

<!-- => Failure["NoProp", ...] -->

---

Under the rules of the prelude, a theorem must state a proposition:

```wl
TypeTheoryEnvironment[TypeTheoryEnvironment[], {
    TypeTheoryInductiveType[Unit, TypeUniverse[1], {UnitIntroduction -> Unit}],
    TypeTheoryTheorem[UnitInhabited, Unit, UnitIntroduction]
}]
```
